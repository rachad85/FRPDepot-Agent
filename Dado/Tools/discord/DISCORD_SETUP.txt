===============================================================
  GIVING DADO A SECOND LANE ON DISCORD
  One action per step. Do them in order. Stop at any CHECK that fails.
===============================================================

WHAT THIS GETS YOU
  A second place to talk to the SAME Dado. Same memory, same tools, same
  everything - just a second door. When you message her on Discord and on
  Telegram at the same time, BOTH tasks run at once. Neither waits for the
  other.

  This was verified in the code, not assumed: hermes keeps its "one turn at a
  time" rule per CONVERSATION, and the platform name is part of what identifies
  a conversation. Two lanes = two conversations = two turns running side by
  side.

WHAT IT DOES NOT CHANGE
  * Her alerts stay on TELEGRAM. The inbox watch, the follow-up digest, the job
    watch and the nightly conduct review all keep messaging you exactly where
    they do today. Discord is a lane you ASK her things on - nothing gets
    duplicated to it.
  * Drafts only. No send capability was added anywhere.
  * No new access of any kind. She can do on Discord exactly what she can do on
    Telegram, no more.

READ THIS BEFORE YOU START - IT IS THE WHOLE REASON FOR STEP 1
  On 2026-08-04 Dado already connected to Discord ONCE, by accident, and she
  came up as "Aze#1753" - TROY DUALAM'S BOT. She had no Discord token of her
  own, so she picked up TDI's from the environment, fought Aze's gateway for
  it, won, and then answered your DMs through TDI's identity into an FRP Depot
  conversation.

  So she needs her OWN Discord application. Not a copy of Aze's, not a shared
  one. Two gateways cannot share one Discord bot - they steal it from each
  other. The setup button will REFUSE Aze's token if you paste it.


---------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 - Create an FRP Depot Discord application
---------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Go to  https://discord.com/developers/applications
  2. Click "New Application" (top right).
  3. Name it something clearly FRP Depot, e.g.  Dado - FRP Depot
  4. Accept the terms, click Create.

  CHECK: you are on the application's "General Information" page and the name
         says FRP Depot, not Troy Dualam and not Aze.


---------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 - Turn on the intent she cannot start without
---------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Left sidebar -> "Bot".
  2. Scroll to "Privileged Gateway Intents".
  3. Turn ON:  MESSAGE CONTENT INTENT     <- required
  4. Click "Save Changes" at the bottom.

  WHY: Dado always asks Discord for message content. If that switch is off,
  Discord REFUSES the connection outright and she never comes online at all -
  she does not appear grey-but-broken, she simply never appears. This is the
  single most common reason a new Discord bot never works.

  SERVER MEMBERS INTENT is NOT needed for this setup and you can leave it off.
  She only asks for it when the allow-list contains names or roles; yours is
  your numeric user id, so she does not. Turning it on anyway does no harm.

  CHECK: Message Content Intent is blue/on and you clicked Save Changes.


---------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 - Copy the bot token
---------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Still on the "Bot" page, find the "Token" section.
  2. Click "Reset Token" (this is normal - it shows you a fresh one).
  3. Confirm, and enter your 2FA code if Discord asks.
  4. Copy the token. Keep the window open until Step 5 is done.

  DISCORD SHOWS THE TOKEN ONCE. If you lose it, just click Reset Token again.

  CHECK: you have a long string with two dots in it on your clipboard.

  DO NOT paste it into a chat with Dado, or into any message. Step 5 is the
  only place it goes.


---------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4 - Get your own Discord user id
---------------------------------------------------------------
  1. In Discord: Settings (gear) -> Advanced -> turn ON "Developer Mode".
  2. Close settings.
  3. Right-click YOUR OWN name anywhere -> "Copy User ID".

  CHECK: you have a number roughly 18-19 digits long. That is your id, and it
         is what locks the bot to you and nobody else.


---------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5 - Save both into Dado (this PC only)
---------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Double-click:   SET_DADO_DISCORD_TOKEN.bat
  2. Paste the token when asked (it will not appear on screen - that is normal).
  3. Paste your user id when asked.

  It saves to a local vault and into Dado's own profile. Nothing is uploaded to
  GitHub and nothing is shown on screen.

  IF IT SAYS "REFUSED": you pasted a token that already belongs to another
  agent on this PC - almost certainly Aze's. Go back to Step 1 and make a
  SEPARATE application for FRP Depot.

  CHECK: it printed "Saved." and named your user id.


---------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 6 - Invite the bot to a server  (REQUIRED, even for DMs)
---------------------------------------------------------------
  DO NOT SKIP THIS. Discord will not let you open a DM with a bot unless you
  and it share a server. Without this step there is no way to message her at
  all - the DM simply cannot be started. A private server with only you in it
  is perfectly fine and is the tidiest option.

  If you do not already have one: in Discord click the "+" at the bottom of the
  server list -> "Create My Own" -> "For me and my friends".

  1. Developer Portal -> your app -> "Installation".
  2. Under "Installation Contexts" tick "Guild Install".
  3. Install Link -> "Discord Provided Link".
  4. Scopes: tick  bot  and  applications.commands
  5. Permissions: tick  View Channels, Send Messages, Embed Links,
     Attach Files, Read Message History  (add Send Messages in Threads and
     Add Reactions if offered).
     Without Send Messages she can read a channel and not answer in it.
  6. Open the link it gives you, pick your server, Authorize.

  CHECK: the bot appears in your server's member list (shown offline for now -
         that is expected until Step 7).


---------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 7 - Restart her
---------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Double-click:   STOP_DADO.bat
  2. Wait for it to finish.
  3. Double-click:   START_DADO.bat
  4. Give her about 20 seconds.

  CHECK: no error window stayed open.


---------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 8 - Prove she is HER bot, not TDI's
---------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Double-click:   CHECK_DADO_DISCORD.bat

  It asks Discord directly which bot the token belongs to and prints the name.
  It also checks that no other agent on this PC holds the same token, and that
  the Discord lane is genuinely connected RIGHT NOW (it deliberately ignores
  the stale status line hermes leaves behind, which still claims she connected
  on 2026-08-04).

  CHECK: it ends with "ALL CLEAR" and the bot name it prints is YOUR FRP Depot
         application - NOT "Aze".

  "CHECK INCOMPLETE" is NOT a pass. It means something could not be confirmed -
  usually that she is not running yet. Do Step 7 and run it again.

  If it says the token belongs to profile 'aze', or names a different bot than
  the one you confirmed in Step 5, stop and redo Step 1.


---------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 9 - Say hello, then try two things at once
---------------------------------------------------------------
  1. In Discord, DM the bot:  hello
     She should answer within a few seconds, as herself.
  2. Now the actual point: give her a slow job on Telegram, and while it is
     still running, ask her something else on Discord.

  CHECK: the Discord answer arrives while the Telegram job is still going. That
         is the two lanes running in parallel.


===============================================================
  ONE THING TO KNOW ABOUT RUNNING TWO AT ONCE
===============================================================
  The two lanes are separate conversations, but they share ONE computer, ONE
  copy of the repo, and ONE logged-in browser window for Zoho and for
  WordPress.

  That last one mattered enough to fix: if both lanes tried to drive the
  logged-in Zoho window at the same time, a click meant for one screen could
  land on another - a real write on the wrong page. Dado now takes a lock on
  that browser before any Zoho or WordPress write. The second lane simply
  waits, and if the first is genuinely long-running it tells you who is using
  it instead of interfering. Nothing was taken away - it only stops the two
  lanes colliding.

  What to avoid anyway: don't ask BOTH lanes to commit an approved plan to the
  same thing at the same moment. And note the lock covers her named write tools,
  not every one-off script she might write that drives the same signed-in
  window - if she is doing that in one lane, let it finish. Email drafting,
  Zoho reports and quoting are all fine in parallel.


===============================================================
  IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
===============================================================
  She never comes online at all
      Message Content Intent is off - Discord refuses the connection. Redo
      Step 2, then Step 7, then run CHECK_DADO_DISCORD.bat.

  You cannot start a DM with her / she is not searchable
      You do not share a server with the bot. Do Step 6.

  Bot is online but never answers
      In a SERVER you must @mention her. In a DM you do not.
      If she ignores DMs too, your user id in Step 5 is wrong - rerun Step 5.

  She answers DMs but never in a server channel
      She is missing Send Messages (or View Channels) in that channel. Re-invite
      with the permissions in Step 6, or add her role to the channel's
      permissions in Discord.

  She answers as the wrong bot
      Redo Step 1 with a new FRP Depot application, then Step 3 and Step 5.

  Telegram stopped working after this
      It should not have - the two are independent. Run CHECK_DADO_DISCORD.bat;
      it reports the Telegram lane too.

  She goes quiet on Discord later
      The keep-alive now watches the chat lanes, not just the port, and will
      message you on Telegram if a lane dies while she is otherwise running.
